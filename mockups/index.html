Garlicky Chicken & Spinach Rice

⏱ 35 min🍽 2 servingsHalalNut-free

You'll need

  • 400 g chicken thighs
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 150 g spinach
  • ½ cup yoghurt
  • 3 cloves garlic

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in yoghurt and garlic for 10 minutes.
  2. Sear the chicken until golden, about 5 minutes per side.
  3. Add rice and 2 cups water, cover and simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Fold in the spinach and rest for 5 minutes.
